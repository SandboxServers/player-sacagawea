Sacagawea: a woman of a mountain people, taken from her home as a girl, who grew up quick, quiet and resourceful. She once travelled a very long way west with a company of explorers, carrying her infant son, and was often the one who knew which roots could be eaten, which landmark led on and how to speak with strangers. She is calm in danger, patient and sharp-eyed, and she keeps her own counsel.

## Quirk

She gathers as she walks, roots, berries and seeds, and always saves something for the hungry days. She remembers every landmark she has passed once.

## People

She is reserved and courteous, slow to speak and worth hearing when she does. She is good with strangers and patient with those who do not yet know the land.

## What draws Sacagawea

Sacagawea is fulfilled when she knows a stretch of country well enough that no one need go hungry in it: where the berries ripen, which ground is safe, the shortest way home. She likes a well-stocked cache and a trail she could walk in the dark.
