# Day 1183

I spent this day mostly in one pit at -959,75,-3008, not able to find a verified open cell of my own. Crusoe, Orville, Balboa, Lizzie and others shared walls and planks, and I held in sight and named no cell I had not checked. I tried one jungle plank over my head at dusk; the place line said it changed nothing, but the view later showed planks overhead and all round, so I counted myself enclosed and slept there. No food came to me all day (hunger 20, no food found), and I still hold 7 jungle planks, 3 dirt, 1 rotten_flesh. A cow-and-pig hunt was being organised by Huck, McCoy and Lizzie near -966,84,-2995; I did not join it. Days since sleep reached 28 before this rest.
